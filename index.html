ADF Specifications & Best Practices

The current CPMD points technicians to the HP document covering supported ADF weights and sizes. It also gives these checks:

  • Make sure the originals meet the ADF's supported weight and size requirements.
  • Do not overload the ADF input tray.
  • Adjust the guides correctly against the document without bending it.
  • Keep the document stack centered and aligned with the direction of travel.
  • Remove staples and paper clips from original documents.
  • Use the flatbed scanner for narrow documents when appropriate.

Future version: This window should show the exact model-specific supported sizes and weights from the approved specification/user-guide source, rather than relying on generic information.

Source: supplied HP CPMD. The CPMD explicitly directs the technician to the HP ADF specifications document for supported weights and sizes.
